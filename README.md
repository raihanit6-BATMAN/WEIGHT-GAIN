# 🏋️‍♂️ Weight Gain – Bangladeshi Health & Nutrition Guide

A localized, progressive web application (PWA) tailored specifically for hardgainers and underweight individuals in Bangladesh. The app helps users track their caloric surplus, macronutrients, and hydration using authentic, affordable, and everyday Bangladeshi food staples rather than costly imported supplements.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **Target-Driven Calorie & Macro Rings:** Real-time visual tracking of daily bulking calorie targets alongside Protein, Carbohydrates, and Healthy Fats.
* **Localized 6-Meal Routine Logger:** Pre-configured routines covering Breakfast, Mid-Morning Snack, Lunch, Afternoon Snack, Dinner, and Bedtime with traditional Bangladeshi food presets.
* **Authentic Deshi Food Database:** Granular portion selections for common items like eggs (pieces), bananas, milk, lentils (patla/bhuna dal in bowls), rice/khichuri, chicken, and local fish.
* **Home Cooking & Oil Adjustments:** Smart estimation presets for vegetable curries (sobji torkari, shak bhaji) with optional cooking oil level adjustments.
* **Outside & Street Snack Estimator:** Quick-pick presets and rough calorie guessing for common street items (singara, somosa, puri, milk tea, bakery items).
* **Daily Hydration Tracker:** Interactive fluid tracker with customizable increments (+250ml, +500ml) to ensure optimal hydration for muscle growth.
* **Personal Health & BMI Assessment:** Baseline metric tracking (weight, height, age) and visual BMI progression mapping the gap to reach a healthy weight.
* **Scheduled Daily Alerts:** Timely reminders at 10:00 AM, 2:00 PM, and 9:00 PM to keep users accountable to their daily nutrition and hydration goals.
* **Offline-Ready PWA:** Installable directly on Android and iOS devices with full standalone display support and persistent `localStorage` management.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Vite
* **Styling:** Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons
* **PWA / Service Worker:** Web App Manifest, Cache API, Notification API
* **Deployment:** Vercel
